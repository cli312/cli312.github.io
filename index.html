Research

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica under the mentorship of Chun-Ju Lai. I was previously a graduate student at Columbia University, under the supervision of Mikhail Khovanov. I am interested in Representation Theory, Categorification, Link Homology and Algebraic Geometry.
